# Parental leave

Birth mothers and primary adopters may take up to 52 weeks of leave. We pay the first 8 weeks at full pay, then statutory pay for the following 31 weeks. The remainder is unpaid.

Partners may take 3 weeks of leave at full pay, to be used within 12 weeks of the birth or placement.

Tell Tomas Weir at least 15 weeks before the expected week of birth, using form HR-09. You may work up to 10 keeping-in-touch days during leave, paid at your normal rate.
