# Food safety

## Fridge temperatures

Fridges must stay at 5°C or below. Check and log them at opening and at 14:00. If a fridge reads between 5°C and 8°C, move the stock to another fridge and check again after 30 minutes. If it reads above 8°C for more than 2 hours, throw the stock away and call your manager.

## Date labels

Milk is used within 3 days of opening. Cut cakes last 2 days. Sandwiches are sold on the day they arrive, and never after their use-by date, even if they look fine.

## Hot food

Riverside's hot food must reach 75°C in the centre, measured with the probe thermometer. Hot food can be held at 63°C or above for up to 2 hours. Food can't be reheated twice.

## Cleaning

Clean food contact surfaces with the blue sanitiser, and leave it for 30 seconds before wiping. Use the red cloths for toilets only and the green cloths for the front of house. Wash your hands for 20 seconds after handling raw food, money or rubbish.

## Pest sightings

Report any droppings, gnawed packaging or live insects to your manager straight away. Don't put down your own traps. Our pest contractor is Halden Pest Control, who visit each branch every 8 weeks.
