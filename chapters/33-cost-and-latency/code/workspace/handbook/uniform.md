# Uniform

We give you two aprons and one cap when you start. Wear dark trousers or a dark skirt, plain tops in black or navy, and closed, non-slip shoes.

After your probation, you can claim a shoe allowance of £40 every 12 months, using form FN-3 with a receipt.

Long hair must be tied back. Keep jewellery to plain rings and small stud earrings. Replacement aprons are free if the old one is worn out, but cost £8 if lost.
