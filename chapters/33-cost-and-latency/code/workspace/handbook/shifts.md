# Shifts and arrival times

Rotas are published in StaffHub every Thursday for the two weeks starting the following Monday.

Opening staff must arrive 30 minutes before the branch opens. Closing staff stay 45 minutes after the branch closes. Everyone else arrives 10 minutes before their shift so that they can get ready.

If you are running late, phone the branch. Arriving more than 15 minutes late three times in a rolling 3-month period leads to a meeting with your branch manager.
