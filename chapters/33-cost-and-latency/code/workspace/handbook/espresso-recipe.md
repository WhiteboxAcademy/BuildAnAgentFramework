# Espresso recipe

Our house blend is Lantern. Use 18 grams of ground coffee to give 36 grams of espresso in 26 to 30 seconds.

If the shot runs fast, grind finer. If it runs slow, grind coarser. Change one thing at a time, and throw away the first shot after any change.

Our decaf is called Dusk. It uses 18 grams in and 38 grams out in 24 to 28 seconds. A flat white uses a double shot and 150ml of milk. A cortado uses a double shot and 60ml of milk.
