# Sickness absence

If you cannot come to work because you are unwell, phone your branch manager at least two hours before your shift starts. Don't send a text or use StaffHub for this.

For an absence of up to 7 calendar days, you complete a self-certification form, HR-04, on your return. After 7 days you need a doctor's note.

Staff with six months of service receive full pay for the first 5 working days of sickness in a leave year. After that, statutory sick pay applies.

Food handlers who have had vomiting or diarrhoea must stay away until 48 hours after symptoms stop.
