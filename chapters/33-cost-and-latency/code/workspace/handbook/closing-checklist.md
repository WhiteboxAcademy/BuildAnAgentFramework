# Closing checklist

1. Lock the door at closing time and let the remaining customers finish.
2. Backflush the group heads with detergent.
3. Soak the portafilters and steam wand tips for 15 minutes.
4. Empty and wash the grinder hoppers on Fridays only.
5. Count the till and tips, and complete the cash sheet.
6. Throw away unsold pastries, and record them on the waste sheet.
7. Empty the bins, mop the floor and check that the back door is locked.
8. Set the alarm. You have 60 seconds to leave.
