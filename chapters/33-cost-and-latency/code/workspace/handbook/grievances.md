# Grievances

If something at work is troubling you, talk to your branch manager first. Most problems can be solved that way. If the problem involves your manager, talk to Tomas Weir.

For a formal grievance, complete form HR-12 and send it to Tomas Weir. You will be invited to a meeting within 5 working days, and you may bring a colleague or union representative. You will receive a written outcome within 5 working days of the meeting. You may appeal to Priya Raman within 10 days.
