# Annual leave

Full-time staff receive 28 days of annual leave each year, including public holidays. Part-time staff receive the same amount in proportion to their contracted hours. After five years of service, you receive 2 extra days.

The leave year runs from 1 April to 31 March. You may carry over up to 5 unused days into the next leave year. Anything above that is lost.

Request annual leave in StaffHub at least 14 days ahead. Requests for December are decided by your branch manager on 1 November.
