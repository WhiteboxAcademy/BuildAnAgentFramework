# Deliveries

Greywell Dairy delivers on Mondays, Wednesdays and Saturdays before 07:00. Pastries from Fennel Bakehouse arrive every day by 06:15. Roasted beans come from the Old Mill roastery every Wednesday.

Check each delivery against its note. Chilled goods must arrive at 8°C or below, so measure one item with the probe thermometer. Refuse anything warmer, or with damaged packaging, and write the reason on the note.

Ordering closes at 14:00 the day before.
