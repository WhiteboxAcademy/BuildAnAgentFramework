# Accidents and incidents

Any injury to a colleague or a customer, however small, must be reported on form IR-7 before the end of the shift. Your branch manager sends it to Ines Duarte within 24 hours.

Near misses count too. A wet floor that almost caused a fall is still an incident worth recording.

If someone is seriously hurt, don't move anything at the scene unless it's unsafe to leave it. Take photographs if you can do so safely. If a customer asks about compensation, don't discuss it. Give them the head office address.
