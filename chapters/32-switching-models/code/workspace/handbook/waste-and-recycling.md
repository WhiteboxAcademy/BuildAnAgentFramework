# Waste and recycling

Coffee grounds go in the brown caddy and are collected every Tuesday by GreenLoop, who turn them into compost. Our takeaway cups go in the separate cup bin, not with general recycling. Milk cartons go in the blue bin once they are rinsed.

Unsold pastries that are still in date may be offered through the Leftover app after 15:00. Anything past its date goes in the food waste bin and on the waste sheet.
