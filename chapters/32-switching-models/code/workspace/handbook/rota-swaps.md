# Swapping a shift

You may swap a shift with a colleague of the same band or higher, as long as the swap doesn't take either person above 48 hours in the week.

Post the swap in StaffHub at least 24 hours before the shift. It is confirmed only when the other person accepts and your manager approves it. Until then, the shift is still yours.

You can't swap an opening shift to a trainee barista, because trainees may not open a branch alone.
