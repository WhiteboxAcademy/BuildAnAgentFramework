# Job roles and bands

Every role belongs to a band.

- Trainee barista: Band A
- Barista: Band B
- Senior barista: Band C
- Shift lead: Band C
- Assistant manager: Band D
- Branch manager: Band E
- Roaster: Band D

You move from trainee barista to barista after passing the Level 1 assessment. Your band appears on your contract and on your payslip.
