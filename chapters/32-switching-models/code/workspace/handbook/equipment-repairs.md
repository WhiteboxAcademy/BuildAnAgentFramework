# Equipment repairs

If equipment breaks, first put an out-of-order sign on it. Then phone the equipment service contractor listed for your branch. Give them the machine's serial number from the plate on the back.

Log every repair call in StaffHub under Maintenance. Repairs under £200 can be approved by a branch manager. Anything over £200 needs Ines Duarte's approval.

Never take off a machine's panels yourself, and don't use a machine that has given you an electric shock.
