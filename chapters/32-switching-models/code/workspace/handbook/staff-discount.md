# Staff discount

The discount applies when you are off shift, with your staff card, at any branch.

- Band A and Band B: 25% off drinks and food, 15% off retail beans
- Band C and Band D: 35% off drinks and food, 25% off retail beans
- Band E: 40% off drinks and food, 30% off retail beans

You may bring one guest, who receives the same discount on drinks only. The discount can't be combined with the loyalty card or used for catering orders.
