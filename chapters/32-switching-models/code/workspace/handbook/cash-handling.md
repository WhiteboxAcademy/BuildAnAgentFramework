# Cash handling

Each till starts the day with a float of £150. Only the shift lead or a manager may open the safe.

Cash differences of more than £5 must be recorded on the cash sheet and reported to your branch manager on the same day. Banking is collected on Mondays and Thursdays by SecureRoute, who will show photo ID. Never hand over cash to anyone else, even if they claim to be from head office.

If you suspect a forged note, politely refuse it and hand it back.
