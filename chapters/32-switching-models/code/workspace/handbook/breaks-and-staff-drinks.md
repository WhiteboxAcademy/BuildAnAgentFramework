# Breaks and staff drinks

A shift of 4 to 6 hours includes one paid 15-minute break. A shift longer than 6 hours adds an unpaid 30-minute meal break. A shift longer than 9 hours adds a second paid 15-minute break.

Staff get one free drink for every 4 hours worked, rounded down. Free drinks don't include bottled drinks or anything made for someone else. Take your break away from the counter.
