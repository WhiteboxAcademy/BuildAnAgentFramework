# Milk and alternatives

Our standard milk is whole milk from Greywell Dairy. We also stock semi-skimmed, oat, soya and coconut.

Oat, soya and coconut cost 45p extra. Semi-skimmed is free. Steam milk to between 60°C and 65°C. Oat milk needs to stop at 60°C or it will split.

Keep plant milks in date order. Oat and soya last 5 days after opening, and coconut lasts 3.
