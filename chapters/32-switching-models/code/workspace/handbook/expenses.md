# Expenses

Claim work expenses on form FN-3 with receipts attached, within 30 days of spending the money. Claims are paid with your next wages.

Travel between branches on a work day is paid at 45p a mile if you drive, or at the actual fare if you use public transport. Travel from home to your normal branch isn't covered. Taxis need your manager's agreement in advance, except when you finish after 22:00.
