# First aid

Every branch has a first aid kit behind the counter and at least one trained first aider on each shift. The first aider is marked on the rota with a green cross.

For a minor burn, cool it under running water for 20 minutes. Don't use ice or creams. For anything serious, call 999 first and then your manager.

Replace anything you use from the kit, and record it on the card inside the lid. Kits are checked on the first Monday of each month.
