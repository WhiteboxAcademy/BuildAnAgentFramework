# Opening checklist

1. Disarm the alarm within 45 seconds of entering.
2. Turn on the espresso machine. It needs 25 minutes to reach temperature.
3. Check the fridge temperatures and record them in the log.
4. Purge the steam wands and flush each group head for 10 seconds.
5. Dial in the grinder using the recipe card, and pour two test shots.
6. Count the float. It should be £150.
7. Put out pastries, with today's date on the labels.
8. Unlock the doors at opening time, not before.
