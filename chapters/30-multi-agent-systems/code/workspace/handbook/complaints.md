# Customer complaints

Listen first, apologise, and try to put it right on the spot. Most complaints end with a remade drink.

If you can't resolve a complaint, write down the customer's name and contact details, and pass them to your branch manager, who must reply within 3 working days. Serious complaints, such as a suspected allergic reaction or food poisoning, go to Ines Duarte the same day.

If a customer is abusive, you don't have to serve them. Call your shift lead. Never argue on social media, however unfair a review seems.
