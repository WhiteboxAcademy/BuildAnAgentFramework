# Tips

Cash tips go into the tip jar and are counted at closing. Card tips are added by the payment system.

All tips are shared each month by hours worked, across everyone at the branch below Band E. Card tips are paid with your wages and cash tips are handed over in an envelope on the first Monday of the month.

Trainee baristas share in tips from their first day. Tips are recorded on your payslip so that tax can be paid on them.
