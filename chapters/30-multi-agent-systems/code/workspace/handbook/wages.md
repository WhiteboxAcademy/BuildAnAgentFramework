# Wages

Payday is the last Friday of each month. Payslips appear in the StaffHub app two days before payday.

Hourly rates:

- Band A: £12.60
- Band B: £13.40
- Band C: £14.75
- Band D: £16.90

Band E is salaried. Sunday hours are paid at time and a quarter. Hours worked on a public holiday are paid at time and a half.

If your pay looks wrong, tell Tomas Weir within 10 days of payday.
