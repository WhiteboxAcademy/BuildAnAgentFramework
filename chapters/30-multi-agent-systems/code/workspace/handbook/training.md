# Training

Everyone starts with a two-day induction at Riverside. After that, you complete the Level 1 assessment within 8 weeks of joining. It covers espresso, milk, the till and food safety.

Level 2 covers latte art, dialling in and cupping. You can take it after 6 months, and passing it is required to become a senior barista. Level 3 is for shift leads, and covers cash, rotas and handling incidents.

Training time is paid. If you fail an assessment, you can retake it after 2 weeks.
