# Leaving Quillon

To resign, give your branch manager written notice. Band A and Band B staff must give 2 weeks. Band C and Band D staff must give 4 weeks. Band E staff must give 8 weeks.

Your last payslip includes payment for any annual leave you have earned but not taken. Return your apron, keys and any concourse pass on your last day.

You may ask for a reference by emailing Tomas Weir. We confirm your job title and dates of employment only.
