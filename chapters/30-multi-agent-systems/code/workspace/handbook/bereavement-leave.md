# Bereavement leave

If a close relative dies, you may take up to 5 days of paid leave. A close relative means a partner, child, parent or sibling. For other relatives or close friends, you may take 1 paid day to attend the funeral.

You don't need to fill in any form before going. Phone your branch manager as soon as you can. If you need more time, your manager and Tomas Weir will agree it with you.
