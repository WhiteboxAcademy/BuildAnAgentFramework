# Welcome to Quillon Coffee

Quillon Coffee is a small, independent chain with four branches: Harbour, Riverside, Station and Old Mill. The first branch opened at Old Mill in 2011. We roast our own beans at the Old Mill roastery every Tuesday and Friday.

This handbook is for everyone who works with us. It covers pay, leave, daily routines, equipment and what to do when something goes wrong. If anything here is unclear, ask your branch manager first.

The head office team is small. Priya Raman is the managing director, Tomas Weir runs people and payroll, and Ines Duarte runs operations.
